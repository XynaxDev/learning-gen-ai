CHRISTOPHER NOLAN - ALL MOVIES IN NUTSHELLS

1. Following (1998)
A young writer follows strangers around London for inspiration, but gets drawn into the criminal underworld when he befriends a charismatic burglar. Neo-noir thriller shot in black and white with a non-linear narrative.

2. Memento (2000)
A man with short-term memory loss uses notes and tattoos to hunt for his wife's killer. The story is told in reverse chronological order, putting the audience in the protagonist's disoriented perspective.

3. Insomnia (2002)
A veteran detective travels to Alaska to investigate a murder but struggles with guilt and sleeplessness under the midnight sun. A psychological thriller remake starring Al Pacino and Robin Williams.

4. Batman Begins (2005)
Bruce Wayne's origin story as he transforms from a traumatized billionaire into Gotham's masked vigilante. Nolan's gritty, realistic take rebooted the Batman franchise.

5. The Prestige (2006)
Two rival magicians in Victorian London become obsessed with creating the ultimate illusion, leading to dangerous obsession and sacrifice. A twisty tale about rivalry, sacrifice, and the cost of greatness.

6. The Dark Knight (2008)
Batman faces his greatest challenge yet: the Joker, an anarchist who wants to prove that anyone can be corrupted. Heath Ledger's iconic performance elevated the superhero genre to new heights.

7. Inception (2010)
A thief who steals secrets from people's dreams is given a final job: plant an idea in someone's mind instead. Mind-bending heist film with dreams within dreams and that spinning top ending.

8. The Dark Knight Rises (2012)
Eight years after taking the fall for Harvey Dent, Batman returns to save Gotham from the terrorist Bane and complete his journey as the Dark Knight.

9. Interstellar (2014)
A team of astronauts travels through a wormhole near Saturn to find a new home for humanity as Earth becomes uninhabitable. Epic space odyssey exploring love, time dilation, and survival.

10. Dunkirk (2017)
The evacuation of Allied soldiers from Dunkirk beach during WWII, told from three perspectives: land, sea, and air. Intense war film with minimal dialogue and Hans Zimmer's relentless score.

11. Tenet (2020)
A secret agent learns to manipulate time flow to prevent World War III. Palindromic action thriller featuring "time inversion" where people and objects move backwards through time.

12. Oppenheimer (2023)
The story of J. Robert Oppenheimer and the creation of the atomic bomb during the Manhattan Project, followed by his security clearance hearing. Epic biographical drama that won 7 Academy Awards including Best Picture.

---

Notable Themes Across Nolan's Work:
- Non-linear storytelling
- Time manipulation
- Obsession and sacrifice
- Moral ambiguity
- Practical effects over CGI
- Complex narratives requiring multiple viewings
- Collaboration with composer Hans Zimmer and cinematographer Hoyte van Hoytema
